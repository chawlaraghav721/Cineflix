## Coverage and interpretation

There are 20 movies and 10 shows. Nineteen entries have a researched original
summary, principal cast, director information and a publisher-confirmed trailer
reference. Eleven identities remain unresolved. Their `description`, `director`
and `trailerUrl` are `""`, and `cast` is `[]`.

TV directing credits are explicitly limited to a season or episodes where that is
what the source establishes. A creator credit alone was not used as evidence of
direction. These are useful directing credits, not necessarily exhaustive lists
of everyone who has directed the series. Spirited Away lists the Japanese voice
cast; its promotional trailer is the official English-language trailer.

## External asset verification limits

- All 19 stored trailers passed live oEmbed requests with matching publishers and
  embed markup. This confirms identity and embed publication, not uninterrupted
  playback in every browser, region or account.
- Wednesday's longer trailer candidate `Q73UhUTs6y0` returned HTTP 403; it was
  excluded in favor of the accessible official 2022 teaser.
- All 30 poster URLs were requested. Twenty-nine returned HTTP 200 with image
  content types and image signatures. `show-006` failed DNS resolution for
  `www.moviedigger.it`; its established URL remains unchanged.
- Successful image delivery does not establish poster identity. The Unsplash
  references are generic illustrations, including the first ten movies and some
  shows. Replacing them or resolving the ambiguous entries requires curated
  production identity decisions beyond attaching verified metadata.
